Glass Garden · TestFlight 0.5.0(20)

This section applies to Pourlune Glass Garden 0.5.0(20) for TestFlight, when that build is made available. It does not change the description of the earlier60-level builds below. Pourlune is operated by changjiang li; privacy/support contact: wangyule0379@gmail.com.

Glass Garden stores puzzle boards, move history, completion records, settings, statistics choices and pending reward/fullscreen receipts locally. Core puzzles and the free Hint, Undo and Restart work offline. There is no player account, cloud sync, payment or subscription. Earlier local campaign records are retained during an update and are not converted into the new campaign. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local records.

Optional GameMunchies statistics start off. If you allow the current notice, the service can receive a random installation identifier, event identifiers and first-occurrence times, active-use/new-round events and ad opportunity, load, failure, impression and earned-reward outcomes. Ad event context includes the placement, provider, app version and attempt identifiers. These events do not include your name, email, puzzle board or an advertising ID from this app. Event processing uses the registered game, platform and environment. The new campaign's reporting installation identifier is independent of the older campaign's reporting identity.

GameMunchies configuration requests are independent of optional statistics and can occur while statistics is off. They communicate the game, platform and ordinary network information, including the IP address, to the service and its infrastructure. Remote settings can disable eligible placements or tighten the local rules; they do not revoke a saved reward or replace Google's advertising-consent checks.

The new campaign uses a separate local statistics store. The older campaign's installation identifier and pending statistics are not imported or replayed into that store; the original storage remains on the device. This can interrupt reporting identity/continuity across the campaign change. It is not a promise that every old or interrupted observation will be delivered.

The current JavaScript SDK bounds its local pending queue to1000 events/512KiB and discards entries older than30 days during subsequent queue processing. Storage eviction, interruption or failures can lose pending telemetry. Turning Analytics off stops new optional statistics, attempts to cancel event requests and clears the current service scope's pending queue. The local installation identifier can remain. It cannot recall data already received by the server. Later allowing does not backfill raw observations captured while sharing was off; valid events already queued under permission can retry with the same identifiers while sharing remains allowed. Support requests and server/infrastructure retention still follow the applicable sections below; local deletion does not recall already received data.

This candidate does not newly enable Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Firebase Remote Config in the Glass Garden runtime. An earlier saved crash-sharing preference is retained with the older local records but does not turn on a new crash-reporting service here.

The candidate uses official Google test advertisements with Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Test advertisements still involve network requests and Google processing of IP-derived approximate location, app/device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices for delivery, measurement, consent and fraud prevention. Requests use non-personalized mode. The app does not request ATT authorization or Android advertising-ID permission; other SDK identifiers and processing can still exist. A test/non-personalized advertisement is not a promise of no data collection.

Google consent eligibility gates ad loading separately from the Analytics choice. Use Ad privacy options in Settings when Google requires them. Turning Analytics off does not itself turn off UMP or eligible Google test-ad requests. No optional play-statistics permission is inferred from advertising eligibility. An extra bottle requires the actual rewarded-ad confirmation and a successful game save; closing an ad alone does not earn it.

The operator/contact, voluntary feedback, service-provider/international processing, children and website-hosting sections below continue to apply. These pages do not add forms or scripts. A public support page cannot guarantee service delivery or erase provider records. The build uses the service configuration described in this section.

Data on your device

Pourlune stores puzzle progress, undo history, collection pieces and settings locally. Core puzzles and free Hint, Undo and Restart work offline. There is no player account, cloud sync, payment or subscription. Optional online reporting is separate from your local game save. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and keeps preferences. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress. Local reset does not delete records already received by a service provider.

Configured online services

Distributed builds with Firebase configured use the following services. Firebase Remote Config fetches bounded feature flags and disabled puzzle IDs using Firebase installation infrastructure. This can process an installation identifier, IP/network and app/device information, country, language, time zone and version information independently of analytics sharing. Unavailable or invalid values preserve local defaults or the last validated values. Remote values do not change an active puzzle or revoke an earned reward.

Optional gameplay analytics

Analytics is initially off. If enabled, the app sends bounded gameplay events including puzzle ID/version, random local instance/request IDs, completion and effective foreground duration, helper counts, and save/reward/ad outcome categories. The app does not put full boards, email, feedback bodies or arbitrary exception messages in those manual events. SDK technical and automatic events can also exist. Advertising storage, advertising user-data consent, personalization consent and automatic screen reporting are disabled in this implementation. The iOS Analytics dependency is the no-AdID variant; this does not prove that every embedded SDK is silent.

GameMunchies statistics and ad settings

GameMunchies is not enabled in iOS builds 16 and 17 or Android closed-test build 13. Starting with version 0.5.0(18), when configured, the app connects to our GameMunchies service at api.gamemunchies.com. Optional gameplay statistics remain off initially and require your saved Analytics choice. Separate integration-test builds use a separate test game and environment; their records are not copied into production statistics.

The SDK records activity and newly started puzzle instances. From SDK 0.2.0 in iOS and Android build 19, optional ad statistics also record display opportunities, blocked reasons, load requests and outcomes, show attempts and failures, actual impressions, and actual reward callbacks. Resuming a saved puzzle, returning to the foreground or adding a bottle does not create a new-round event. Each event contains a random installation identifier, event identifier, event type, environment label and first occurrence time. The request also identifies the registered game and platform. Ad observations include ad format, in-game placement, provider, app version, random load/opportunity correlation identifiers and bounded failure or blocking categories. These events do not include your email, name, advertising ID, puzzle number, board contents, raw SDK error text or reward amount. Network infrastructure handles connection information such as your IP address; the service uses IP information for rate limiting but does not put it in the gameplay-event record.

GameMunchies also fetches bounded ad settings independently of Analytics: whether advertisements and the existing next-level and extra-bottle scenes are enabled, and the interstitial interval. These requests use the registered game, platform and environment and involve network/security processing even while optional statistics are off. The app does not create a GameMunchies installation identifier solely to fetch settings. Cached or default settings allow offline puzzles to continue. Settings cannot revoke an already earned reward or bypass local consent and safety limits.

The SDK stores an installation identifier and a bounded pending queue on the device, with a maximum of 1,000 events or 512 KiB. Events older than 30 days are discarded during subsequent queue processing. Server cleanup targets records older than 30 days and runs in batches, so deletion is not guaranteed at the exact age boundary. Infrastructure security logs have separate handling. From version 0.5.0(18), turning Analytics off closes new collection and sending and clears the current service scope's pending queue. The app attempts to cancel in-flight statistics requests; it cannot recall records already received by the server. The stored installation identifier can remain, and Reset Progress does not clear this reporting storage. Previously collected events cleared on withdrawal are not resent when you turn Analytics on again. Contact us about access or deletion; do not send passwords or account tokens.

Optional crash sharing

Crash sharing is a separate choice, initially off. Native crash information may be cached locally while sharing is off. Enabling sharing requests upload of unsent reports, which can include reports cached before the choice was enabled. Diagnostics include technical stack traces and app/device information. Turning sharing off stops new app upload requests and requests deletion of unsent reports. It cannot recall reports already queued or sent, and SDK calls do not provide delivery or deletion receipts.

Change your choices

Use Settings to change analytics and crash sharing independently. A failed local save is shown with Retry and blocks new app event sharing until the choice is saved. A provider synchronization failure is also shown with Retry; other enabled reporting services may continue under your saved choices. Missing or invalid Firebase configuration disables Firebase services, independently of GameMunchies. Turning Analytics off does not disable remote settings or all network processing.

Advertising and consent

When an ad-enabled version is used, Google Mobile Ads requests and displays advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent eligibility and presents applicable privacy choices. The iOS and Android build 19 release configurations use Pourlune's own AdMob units. Separate QA builds and earlier ad-enabled iOS builds use official Google test units. In Android closed-test build 13, ad requests and UMP checks are disabled; that older build can grant one extra buffer per puzzle instance in a local save transaction without pretending an advertisement was watched. In ad-enabled builds, an extra bottle requires the actual rewarded-ad confirmation and a successful local save; closing an ad alone does not grant a reward.

Google's advertising and consent services can process IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, consent management and fraud prevention. The exact identifiers depend on the platform, permissions and consent. Android build 19 removes both the Google Play services and platform AdServices advertising-ID permissions; iOS uses no-AdID Firebase Analytics and does not request ATT tracking authorization. These restrictions do not remove app-set IDs, Firebase installation IDs or other SDK identifiers and data processing. A non-personalized or test ad is not a promise of zero data collection.

The app requests non-personalized advertisements and blocks requests until UMP reports that ads may be requested. Where UMP requires privacy options, they remain available through the app's Settings. Analytics and Crash sharing are separate choices; switching them off does not itself switch off consent checks or eligible ad requests. Ad eligibility is also not treated as permission to enable optional gameplay analytics.

Unavailable consent, inventory or network does not block core puzzles or free Hint, Undo and Restart. An ad-based Extra Bottle reward requires the SDK's actual earned-reward callback and a successful local save; closing an advertisement alone is not a reward. SDK and store privacy links below explain Google's processing and choices.
