Glass Garden · TestFlight 0.5.0(20)

Glass Garden has100 built-in levels and a floral Home, Levels and Settings. The earlier campaign has60 main puzzles, daily puzzles and collections; use the earlier guide below for that version. Test availability depends on the build you have actually received.

Tap a bottle, then an empty bottle or a bottle with the same top color. Small bottles hold four layers. Sort the colors into full bottles. In collector levels, gather all pink water into the fixed tall collector, which accepts pink only, then sort the remaining colors. Covered bottles cannot pour or receive. Finishing a full bottle in the color on a seal lifts the corresponding covers automatically.

Undo, Hint and Restart are free. A hint points to a move without pouring it. A timeout means the search did not finish in time; it does not prove the puzzle is impossible. You can cancel a search, Undo, Restart or keep looking. There is no timer.

Home and Continue reopen the saved board and move history. Completed levels remain available to replay; Next preserves a different unfinished level's saved board. At the end of level100, use Levels or Home. There is no level101.

In an ad-enabled build, Extra bottle requires a genuine earned-reward confirmation and a successful local save. Closing an advertisement alone does not earn a bottle. One ordinary empty bottle can be earned per puzzle. Undo and Restart keep an already earned bottle for that puzzle; replaying a completed level starts a new puzzle. A network failure or unavailable advertisement does not block normal puzzles or the free tools.

Progress and settings are stored on this device. Keep the app open if a save error appears and use Retry save before leaving. There is no player account or cloud sync. Removing the app or clearing app data may remove progress. During the change from the older campaign, its original local records are retained; its level numbers and completed records are not mapped into Glass Garden. The new campaign starts at level1. This does not make the old campaign playable inside the new campaign.

Use Settings for Sound, Music, Haptics where supported, Reduced motion and Analytics. Ad privacy options appear when required by Google's consent system. Report a problem through TestFlight feedback or wangyule0379@gmail.com. Include the actual app version/build, device/OS, level, steps and any save error. The app does not automatically send a board or email. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

How to play · Saves · Daily puzzles · Report a problem

How to play

Tap a source bottle, then a legal destination. Fill every target bottle with the color on its fixed tag and leave all buffer bottles empty. Tags stay fixed but allow another color to enter temporarily while you solve the puzzle.

Hint, Undo and Restart are free. Hint suggests a move without pouring for you. A hint can time out; that does not prove the puzzle is impossible. If no suggestion appears, you can still Undo, Restart or continue yourself.

Save and resume

Core play works offline. Each accepted action saves locally. If saving fails, keep the app open and use Retry. Wait for a successful save before continuing. Main and daily puzzles have separate local saves.

There is no player account or cloud sync. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data or switching devices may lose progress. Reset Progress removes gameplay records and keeps preferences. It does not recall data already received by online services.

Daily puzzles and collections

Daily play follows your device's local date and selects from 30 built-in candidates; it does not download 30 new puzzles each day. When a new date is available, use the in-app choice to keep or replace the saved daily puzzle. Daily Replay does not grant the same daily reward again. Scene pieces come from main progress across 60 main puzzles and three scenes.

Settings and online services

Sound, music, haptics and reduced motion can be adjusted in Settings. Color tags have symbols as well as color. Analytics and crash sharing are separate choices and initially off. Remote settings can still use network infrastructure; see the privacy policy. Starting with 0.5.0(18), when GameMunchies is configured, its optional statistics follow the saved Analytics choice while ad-setting requests remain independent. Builds 16 and 17 do not contain GameMunchies.

Advertising depends on your version. iOS TestFlight 0.5.0 builds 16 and 17 use official Google test ads and consent checks; Android closed-test 0.5.0(13) has advertising disabled. In ad-enabled builds, Extra bottle offers a rewarded ad and grants a bottle only after the actual reward callback is saved. In the current ad-disabled Android build, it grants one bottle directly per puzzle instance. Hint, Undo and Restart stay free. See Advertising and consent for data practices and choices.

Report a problem

After you have received a test invitation, use TestFlight Send Beta Feedback or the Google Play private testing feedback option. You can also email the support contact below. The app never sends a board or feedback automatically.

  • App version and build, device model, and OS version.
  • Main level ID or the daily date shown in the app.
  • Steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened. Include the exact save error if one is shown.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, private account documents or unrelated photos. If you choose to attach a screenshot through the testing platform, check it for personal information first.
